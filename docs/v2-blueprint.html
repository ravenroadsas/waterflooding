Schema check

Your schema is compatible with v2

All six source tables in the .txt load into v2. Two load as they are, two need a column mapping, and two are new tables that v2 adds to the engine. The other four tables are results: pattern rates, velocity, maturity and injection by unit. v2 calculates those itself, and if you upload them it checks its numbers against yours.

Notes on the .txt itself

  • Declare the keys: Wells(Well, Date), Alloc(Well, Pattern, Date), Vol(Pattern, Sand), Fluids(Reservoir), InjSand(Well, Sand, Date).
  • Alloc.Date and InjSand.Date are valid-from dates, so the link to Wells.Date is "latest value on or before the month" rather than an equality join. v2 joins them that way.
  • Units are not declared. v2 assumes STOIIP in stb, HCPV in reservoir barrels and calendar-day rates, and can apply a scale factor (question 6).
  • Vol.Sand and InjSand.Sand must use the same unit names. v2 lists any mismatch on the Data page.
  • If volumetrics or PVT get revised, add a ValidFrom column to Vol and Fluids so history uses the values valid at each date.

App structure

The navigation follows the macroprocess

The top bar becomes a three-step path, in the order the methodology prescribes. Executed jobs come back as evidence, which closes the loop. The drill-down becomes Pattern 360, a side panel reachable from any chart that follows the deductive order Field › Area › Pattern › Well › Unit.

STEP 1Pattern maturityWhere each pattern and unit sits in its flood life, and whether it recovers what it should at that DWI.
STEP 2Process velocityWhether water goes in and fluid comes out at the right speed for that maturity, and whether the pattern is balanced.
STEP 3OpportunitiesWhich interventions maturity and velocity support together, with evidence, alternatives and an expected response.
LOOPIntervention → responseExecuted jobs are evaluated on the same variables that justified them, then feed the next review.

Evidence status

  • screening_only: one family of evidence.
  • candidate: two or more families, including maturity or velocity.
  • validated_candidate: every validation item confirmed by an engineer.
  • executed: linked to an intervention.
  • outcome_evaluated: response window elapsed, expected vs actual stored.

Conformance ranking

  • Method 1 scores each pattern against its area average on utilization (average and cumulative), WOR, water cut, DWI and vertical efficiency. The paper does not define vertical efficiency, so v2 proposes the overlap between injection and HCPV shares by unit (1 = injection in proportion to pore volume).
  • Method 2 plots Evol(MB)/Evol(FF) against Loss = DWI − DTP, split at 0.8 and 0.2 as in SPE-190314 figure 25: thief zone, out of zone or area, or both.

Before implementation

Open questions

The first four change how the engine is built. The rest can be settled during the build.
