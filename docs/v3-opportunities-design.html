FloodPulse · module 3 redesign · analysis before code

Well-centric opportunities

Every opportunity ends as a job on a well. Patterns, single-well analysis and field reviews are lenses that produce evidence. The well, its unit and its interval are the target that is ranked, validated, executed and evaluated. The same module serves waterflooded, primary and other-method fields; a lens is switched on only when its data and drive mechanism are present.

1 · What changes

Diagnosis of the current module

Today (v2)

  • Key is type|pattern|sand. The pattern is the target.
  • Rules A–F need injection, a flood start and prototypes. A primary field produces no opportunities.
  • The well is optional. Rules C and E can leave it empty; others attach the dominant injector.
  • Gain is a waterflood-fit estimate. There is no forecast range, no water forecast, no interval depth.
  • Rule D (ADPERF support) looks at logged ADPERF jobs only. It cannot see new intervals that are still candidates.

Proposed (v3)

  • Key is action|well|unit|interval. A pattern finding is projected onto the wells that carry it.
  • Three lenses produce findings: PATTERN WELL FIELD. Findings on the same target merge into one opportunity with several families of evidence.
  • The drive mechanism per field and unit switches lenses and rules on or off.
  • Gain comes from the Bajo/Base/Alto monthly profiles where they exist, otherwise from the waterflood fit or a decline fit.
  • Rule D becomes support evidence attached to well-interval opportunities.

4 · Drive mechanism

Lenses switch on from data and drive

A small optional table Drive (Field, Unit, Mechanism, Start) declares how each field or unit is produced: primary, waterflood, polymer, gas, steam, other. Without it the app infers waterflood from Baseline and injection, and primary otherwise. A rule runs only when its data are present and its drive matches.

5 · Projection and merge

How pattern findings reach a well

  1. Injector actions (A, B, E-injector, F): target the injector with the largest allocated contribution to the pattern, and the unit from InjSand. Same as today's dominant injector, but the well becomes mandatory.
  2. Producer actions (C, E-producer): target the producer with the largest pattern share of withdrawal, and among those the one with the highest HI or DFL. A pattern with no qualifying producer gives a finding without a target. It shows under "unassigned findings" and cannot be validated.
  3. Support (D): for each W1 candidate, look up the well's patterns in Alloc and the unit's DWI and TP there. Support above the unit percentile adds family V. High DWI with low sw_actual adds a consistency warning, because the interval may already be swept.
  4. Merge: findings with the same action|well|unit|interval become one opportunity; families are the union. Findings on the same well with different actions stay separate opportunities, then group into one job package per well.

Worked example from the attached table

OPEN|CIRA1001|B|INT001. INTERVALOS gives qo 150, qw 1,350, qf 1,500 bfpd (BSW 90 %), estado cerrado. W1 creates the finding with family U (kh above P75) and M (np_ooip_ratio low). CIRA1001 is 60 % in pattern P07, where unit B has TP 9 %/yr and DWI 0.42, so rule D adds V. That gives three families, status candidate. Base profile month 12 gives the 12-month gain; the Bajo–Alto spread (50–300 bopd initial) sets the uncertainty. If rule C also fired on P07 for CIRA1001 (high DFL), a LIFT opportunity joins the same job package.

6 · Ranking and forecast

Score from evidence, profiles and water

score = w_e·n_fam/5 + w_g·gain12/max + w_r·EUR/max − w_u·(Alto−Bajo)/Base − w_w·water12/max, with weights in Settings. Each term reads the best source available for the target:

A risk factor per action and field, P(success), starts at a setting value (0.7) and is replaced by the observed hit rate once at least five outcomes exist. The portfolio shows Base and risked values side by side.

Post-job evaluation

  • Baseline: the well's own pre-job decline (Arps, last 6–12 months), or the waterflood fit for injector jobs, as today.
  • Incremental actual = well test or monthly rate after the job minus the baseline. It is compared month by month with the frozen Base profile.
  • Verdict: above Alto, within the range, below Bajo. The ratio actual/Base per action and unit becomes the calibration factor shown next to future forecasts.

9 · To confirm

Open questions

  1. qa_resultado values. Which values exist, and which ones mean the interval is usable? Proposal: OK passes, anything else caps the opportunity at screening.
  2. estado_apertura. Does INTERVALOS list every interval of the well, including never-perforated ones as cerrado? If so, how do we tell "never opened" (OPEN) from "isolated earlier" (REOPEN)? Proposal: use the Interventions history; without it, call both OPEN.
  3. Incremental vs total. Is qo_inicial the rate of the interval alone, so the well gain is that rate (commingled), or does opening it change the rates of the open intervals? Proposal: treat it as incremental for now.
  4. qw_perfil increment. The image says this method is still to be agreed. Until then the app reads qw_perfil as delivered and only checks qf = qo + qw.
  5. ORGUNIT. Is it above FIELD (asset or business unit), and is it needed as a filter and a join key, or only as a label?
  6. Economics. Do you want a simple value (oil price, lifting cost per barrel of water, cost per job type) in Settings now, or ranking on volumes only for the pilot?
  7. Drive table. Can you provide Field, Unit, Mechanism, Start, or should the app infer the drive from injection data only?
  8. Other lenses. Besides single producer analysis, will there be single injector analysis or field-level studies (infill, conversion) delivering their own tables? The finding layer accepts them, but each needs its own mapping.
