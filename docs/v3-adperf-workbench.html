3 · Portfolio

Rank the job against the other proposals

Each proposal is a job (one or more opportunities on one well). Value comes from the economic function once it is plugged in; until then the screen ranks risked oil. Jobs triggered by an opportunity, such as a lift near the end of its life or a well already down, show the trigger and get the bonus the asset decides.

Score = risked oil per cost, scaled 0-100, × (1 + opportunity bonus 0.25) − 10 × relative uncertainty. Weights and the bonus would be settings; the private economic function replaces risked oil with value when plugged in.

6 · Decisions

Answers that shaped the build

  1. Algorithms return intervals only; potential comes from the single-well analysis.
  2. Completion history by depth and date is available (table Completions).
  3. Water offenders: version 1 is oil and water per interval; version 2 a synthetic PLT with the same columns.
  4. Theoretical potential per interval; potentials add up, and every job has its own potential.
  5. Costs: standard cost lookup by well depth and job type.
  6. Lift data from the CDF system (export table for now).
  7. Approval: engineers propose, a lead approves.
