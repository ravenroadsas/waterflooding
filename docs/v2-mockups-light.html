Step 2

2 · Process velocity

Checks speed against efficiency first, then the change in injectivity over a year, then balance, then overlays throughput on maturity by unit. The result is a rate diagnosis per pattern and unit: accelerate, control, restore injectivity, increase extraction or hold.

  1. KPI ribbon: injection and production TP over 12 months, IWR, utilization, change in TP over a year, rate gap to target, and counts of under- and over-balanced patterns.
  2. Utilization vs Inj TP: low utilization with TP below target means room to accelerate (P04); high utilization with high TP means water cycling that needs control (P12).
  3. TP now vs 12 months ago with the 1:1 line and ±25 % bands. P13 lost 62 % of its throughput in a year.
  4. IWR vs utilization with the 0.8 and 1.2 screening bands. Over-balance with low utilization (P07) points to extraction limits; very low IWR (P13, P10) to depletion risk.
  5. Balance extends the v1 voidage chart: Inj TP vs Prod TP per pattern, with a toggle to DWI vs DTP (the difference is Loss). The field voidage history stays below it.
  6. DWI + TP by unit: DWI as the background (green low, blue high), injector bubbles coloured by unit TP, as in SPE-190314 figure 19. High DWI with high TP is a control zone; low DWI with low TP is an acceleration zone.
  7. Rate gap: current injection vs the rate implied by the target TP for each pattern or unit, as an actionable number in bwipd.
  8. TP by unit over time against the target shows persistent low-injectivity units (D and E here) and sudden restrictions.
  9. Pulse board from v1 gains TP, IWR and utilization as metrics. The v1 production & injection chart and the exception feed sit below, and the feed now lists screening signals that go to step 3.
